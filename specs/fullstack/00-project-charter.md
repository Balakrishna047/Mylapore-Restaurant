# Project Charter: Mylapore Express Restaurant E-Commerce Platform

- **Project Code**: ME-2026
- **Project Name**: Mylapore Express
- **Classification**: Enterprise Culinary E-Commerce & Operational Commerce Platform
- **Lead Architecture Team**: Chief Technology Officer, Enterprise Solution Architect, Principal Full-Stack Engineer, Salesforce Agentforce Architect

## 1. Executive Summary
Mylapore Express brings authentic, blast-chilled, preservative-free South Indian vegetarian culinary heritage (from Chennai's iconic Mylapore neighborhood) to modern digital consumers. Customers can discover traditional dishes, customize preferences (Jain, Vegan, spice tiers, grain variants), place orders, checkout securely via Stripe, track kitchen and delivery progress in real-time, reschedule, request operational cancellations/refunds, and engage with autonomous Agentforce AI concierge support.

## 2. Business Objectives & Key Results (OKRs)
- **Objective 1 (Commercial Velocity)**: Enable seamless multi-item order placement in under 90 seconds with zero price calculation mismatch between frontend and backend.
- **Objective 2 (Operational Precision)**: Eliminate order fulfillment errors through deterministic state transitions and kitchen backlog slot caps.
- **Objective 3 (Customer Experience & Retention)**: Deliver transparent blast-chilled reheatable meal preparation instructions, allergen disclaimers, and 24/7 AI-driven customer assistance.
- **Objective 4 (Enterprise Integration)**: Synchronize customer profiles, order history, and support inquiries with Salesforce Service Cloud and Agentforce AI without exposing sensitive payment credentials.

## 3. Key Stakeholder Personas
- **Consumer**: Seeks traditional, comforting South Indian meals; demands diet transparency (Allergen, Jain, Vegan), flexible delivery windows, and live tracking.
- **Kitchen Manager**: Controls preparation queues, slot capacity, blast-chilling batches, and dish 86'ing (inventory stock-outs).
- **Delivery Dispatcher / Driver**: Coordinates driver assignments, temperature-controlled delivery transit, and proof of drop-off.
- **Customer Support Agent**: Resolves delivery exceptions, address updates, refunds, and inquiries escalated from the AI concierge.
- **Platform Administrator / Super Admin**: Oversees store hours, menu taxonomy, pricing rules, coupons, analytics, and CRM audit logs.
